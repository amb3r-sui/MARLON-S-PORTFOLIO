# Editorial image generation

The portfolio uses three original images created with OpenAI image generation and stored locally as compressed WebP files. No runtime image service or third-party CDN is required.

## Final prompt set

### Workflow mapping

> Minimal editorial photograph, overhead view of a warm off-white studio desk. Two adult hands arrange small blank paper cards into a clear workflow map with fine pencil connector arrows. One muted blue-gray card provides the only color accent. A dark laptop corner and pencil sit near the frame edge. Soft natural window light, restrained shadows, premium operations-design mood, realistic materials, no readable text, no logos, landscape composition.

Output: `public/images/editorial/workflow-mapping.webp`

### Human review

> Minimal editorial photograph of a human approval checkpoint. Close overhead view of hands reviewing a structured paper checklist beside neutral workflow notes on a warm off-white desk. One muted blue-gray accent, soft natural light, realistic paper texture, calm professional mood, no readable text, no logos, landscape composition.

Output: `public/images/editorial/human-review.webp`

### Integration workbench

> Minimal editorial photograph, overhead view of a compact network hub and tidy cables beside a hand-drawn systems integration map on warm neutral paper. Restrained off-white, charcoal, and muted blue-gray palette, soft natural light, precise but human workspace, no readable text, no logos, landscape composition.

Output: `public/images/editorial/integration-workbench.webp`

The Open Graph image is a 1200 by 630 browser capture of the final homepage, saved as `public/og-portfolio.png`.
